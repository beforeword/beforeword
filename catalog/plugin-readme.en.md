**What a reading adds**

AI writes “I understand.” These words are read as understanding. On what grounds?

Paste a phrase, a passage, or an AI response. beforeword instructs AI to preserve the wording, show what a proposed reading adds, and examine the grounds offered for that step. The same scrutiny applies to its own explanation and to beforeword itself.

## Try it

- Apply beforeword to “I understand.” Include what your own explanation adds.
- Compare “I understand.” under the labels “person” and “AI”. Keep the sentence unchanged.
- Examine “The report proves that the system is safe.” What supports the step from the report to that conclusion?

Use it with definitions, captions, self-descriptions, or conclusions drawn from records. Ask for a response in English, Russian, or another language.

## Scope

Choose beforeword where your application offers plugin or skill selection, then send the text to examine. In a chat, request it by name. In Claude Code, the skill is named `/beforeword:read`.

For a single task, say “Apply beforeword to this passage.” For an ongoing conversation, request that scope explicitly. The instruction remains subject to the host's instruction hierarchy and the context available in that conversation. Exact-copy, code-only, and JSON-only requests retain their requested format.

To stop the conversational mode, say “Stop beforeword for subsequent replies.” Disable or remove an installed copy through the application's plugin controls.
