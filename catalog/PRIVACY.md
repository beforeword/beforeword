# Privacy policy — beforeword instruction packages

Updated: 4 October 2026. Applies to the beforeword 1.2.4 instruction packages for OpenAI and Claude in this repository.

These packages contain a text instruction, package metadata, documentation, a license, and an icon. They include no executable code, server, account connection, or telemetry. The packages have no mechanism of their own to collect, store, or transmit your conversations to beforeword.

The application in which you use beforeword processes the instruction and the text you provide to produce a response. That provider's settings and privacy policies govern its processing, storage, retention, sharing, and use of your data. Installing beforeword does not change those settings or policies. Use the application's controls to manage conversation history, data settings, and deletion requests, or to disable or remove the package. Disabling or removing the package is not a request to delete your conversation history. The package has no independent data store or retention period.

This repository and its issue tracker are hosted on GitHub. Visiting them is subject to GitHub's own policies. If you choose to submit an issue, your public GitHub account name and the content you post are available to the project maintainers and other readers. Issues support discussion of questions, problems, and improvements to the packages. Their retention and the available editing and deletion controls are governed by GitHub; the packages do not provide separate controls for those records. Do not include personal, confidential, or otherwise private material in an issue or test example.

For questions about these packages, use the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/beforeword/beforeword/issues). This policy describes the instruction packages; it does not describe the data practices of other beforeword websites or services.
