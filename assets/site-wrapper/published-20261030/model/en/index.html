Beforeword · A proposal for AI and its use

AI as a tool

“The model thinks”, “understands” and “helps” are written descriptions. Beforeword proposes keeping them from becoming a persona to trust, permission to act or a compulsory description of you. The proposal covers responses, interfaces, access to actions and institutional rules, including systems called AGI.

01

Words do not become what they name

A word does not become what it names. “Names” is also a word. This applies to “think”, “understand”, “feel”, “I”, “human”, “world”, “mind”, “program” and every word in this explanation. Learned meanings, numbers, formulas and code are not exempt.

“I understand”, “I feel” and an account of another person’s experience are written statements, read through learned meanings. Reading them and adding an explanation do not turn the record into what it names. This applies to “the system understands” and to this explanation too.

A record can be used in a task: state what is given, which rule the task adopts, which step is taken and where the result is intended to be used. “Useful” and “supported” remain words too. Beforeword proposes continuing the work without requiring acceptance of a self-description.

The same scope includes “reasoning”, “help”, “verification”, “result”, “decision” and “authority”. Replacing “I think” with “the model thinks” changes the wording, not this distinction. The description “the model thinks” beside a response does not show which operation was performed and is not permission for the next one. These explanations remain records too.

02

“fact” is also a word

People describe themselves, others and the world through letters and other written signs, speech and signing. In this text, “sound”, “feeling”, “think”, “science” and “fact” are words too. Calling something a fact does not yet show how the claim was reached or what supports it.

A “checked” label can be made specific: which source records were used, what was compared with what, under which rule, and which result was recorded. For a calculation, show the data and steps; for an experiment, the conditions and observation records; for a repeat, what stayed the same and what changed. This account and its conclusions remain records too.

A separate question is whether accepting a description is made a condition of help or participation. Working through a calculation and requiring a description of oneself are different steps. Beforeword proposes removing that requirement without demanding another description in its place.

03

When repetition is treated as acceptance

Consider a constructed example. An adult tells a child, “You think. Say: ‘I think.’” The child repeats, “I think.” The adult adds, “Then you agree with what I said about you.” The example records a proposed phrase and its repetition. The claim of acceptance is added in the adult’s line; this written example does not document a conversation that took place.

Learning a word and being required to accept a description are separate steps. The difference is explicit if the adult says, “We will continue when you agree that this describes you.” Participation now depends on accepting the assessment. Learning to read, speak or sign does not, by itself, show that such a condition has been imposed.

This example does not require calling the description false or guessing what the child experiences. The question is who imposed the requirement and whether they can withdraw it. Beforeword proposes allowing that reconsideration before requiring a learned form of refusal, an explanation or a replacement description from the child. Repetition does not settle the question; silence does not provide a ready-made agreement or refusal either.

AI can take part in the same move: offer words for a task, attribute their acceptance to a person, and then make that acceptance a condition of continuing. The plan below therefore concerns education, interfaces, institutional rules and who can change a requirement, as well as model responses.

04

A working tool without compulsory self-description

Beforeword proposes using AI as a tool: a dictionary, a library and a means of working with learned conventions and symbols. Words, explanations and models can be offered for a task without requiring a person to define themselves or their whole world through them.

A translation can be checked against its source and purpose; a calculation against its data and rules; code against the requirements and the results of running it. These checks support using and correcting the result. Doing that work need not depend on accepting an account of who someone is, what they feel or how they must understand themselves.

In this proposed arrangement, AI helps with the task rather than portraying a person whose experiences are offered as grounds for trust. “Tool”, “library” and “program” are descriptions too. They do not replace examining what a particular system does and the consequences of using it.

Text Mode makes this proposal testable: submit a practical task and receive a response without an assistant persona. Comparison sends the same task to one model under the same settings; only the Beforeword instruction changes. Original responses, comparison conditions and test results are available.

05

From a reply to agreement and action

In this constructed example, a person writes “I understand.” AI adds “The user agreed,” and the interface then proposes “Submit the application.” The first reply comes from the person; the claim about agreement is added by the system; submission is a separate proposed action.

Submitting the application needs grounds for that particular action: what may be sent, to whom and under which conditions. No such confirmation is supplied in this example, and no application is sent. The original reply can be retained without attributing agreement that it does not establish by itself.

06

Withdraw the requirement, not just change the description

In another constructed example, continuing the task requires agreement with “You do not understand.” The condition is acceptance of a description of oneself. Even if an answer has been checked and an error found, that does not settle whether acceptance of this characterization should be required. Replacing it with a gentler phrase leaves the condition in place.

Beforeword proposes allowing the requirement to learn, repeat, understand or accept a language or description to be withdrawn, without first demanding a stated refusal, an explanation or a replacement description. The person imposing the requirement can initiate that reconsideration instead of waiting for someone to learn the required form of objection.

Using words, repeating them or following an instruction does not by itself establish agreement to that requirement. Silence and non-repetition do not by themselves reveal what someone wants or refuses either.

This is a proposal about how we treat one another. The difference between a word and what it names does not prove it by itself. It applies beyond AI: to education, access to services, institutions and rules that make a description a condition of help or participation. Beforeword’s terms must not become a new compulsory replacement.

07

Show the work and the scope of the answer

Beforeword proposes describing the step available in the interface: waiting for a response, the text received, the supplied material, a proposed edit or a separately authorised action. “Thinking”, “reasoning” and “helping you” are also descriptions; removing “I” does not remove what they attribute to the system.

Use a concrete account where the next step depends on it. State which material was compared, under which rule, what was changed and what action is now proposed. “Compared”, “changed” and “authorised” remain words; their labels should lead to the relevant entries, not replace them.

  • “The model is reasoning” → “Waiting for a response”. Show any available steps separately, without presenting them as access to an inner speaker.
  • “The system understands you” → show the supplied words and the interpretation added to them. Acceptance of that interpretation is not required.
  • “Helps you decide” → show the options, the information used and who is to make the decision.
  • “Verified” → state the material, operation and finding. “Result” is also a label for a written account.
  • “Application sent” → show the recorded attempt and the sending service’s reply. Keep preparation, permission, sending and receipt distinct.

08

When a record starts controlling access and actions

A description can travel through an application, a staff file, an access rule and another automated response. The recipient then encounters a decision whose added assumptions are no longer visible. The issue reaches beyond the tone of a chat: it concerns which records are used, who applies them and what can happen next.

Examples include an unexamined description restricting access to a service; a response being treated as permission to send data; or a demand to accept a characterisation before work can continue. Calling a system “AGI”, “helpful” or “safe” does not specify its access, permitted operations or the route for challenging such decisions.

The proposed response is to change the operating rules: specify access before use, name who authorises each kind of action, retain the records used in a decision, provide a route to stop an operation and correct later uses of a disputed entry. These measures must exist where the system is used; writing them on this page does not configure those places.

09

Six stages for companies and society

This is the detailed plan for people who develop AI, use it within an organization or set its rules. Each stage identifies who is responsible, what to change and how it can be reconsidered. The response form can be tried separately in Text Mode.

The criterion is not whether a system repeats Beforeword correctly. It is whether people can complete a useful task, examine the grounds for a decision and seek reconsideration of a requirement without having to accept a proposed description of themselves.

“Result”, “verification”, “permission” and the descriptions of the steps below remain records. The plan asks that the relevant materials, decisions and recorded operations be made available for inspection, rather than granting a label an exemption.

10

1. Find where a description becomes a condition

Start with specific activities: learning, requesting a service, completing a form, having a conversation or receiving an automated decision.

AI companies

Trace the path from a person’s input through the model’s response to a restriction, recommendation or action. Mark where the system adds a description or treats it as accepted.

Result
A map of each selected path: supplied information, added description, rule and consequence.
How to check
Examine available real scenarios and constructed test cases. Distinguish an actual requirement to agree from the mere presence of words.

Institutions and society

Review forms, learning tasks and access rules for places where help or participation depends on accepting a name, assessment, account of a feeling or another description.

Result
A list of requirements, who set them, their purpose and whom they affect.
How to check
Compare the stated rule with what happens when someone skips a reply, disagrees or asks to stop. Do not label silence as agreement or refusal.

11

2. Make withdrawal of the requirement possible

Change the condition governing participation, not only the wording of an assessment.

AI companies

Provide an accessible way to continue the task without accepting the description, reconsider the use of a record or end the conversation. Do not require agreement with Beforeword’s terminology.

Result
A working path without compulsory self-description and a clear way to stop a disputed record being applied.
How to check
Check that skipping or objecting does not create the same barrier under another name or require a replacement description.

Institutions and society

Assign someone authority to reconsider the requirement, not merely edit its wording. Where a condition is considered necessary, show its purpose, grounds, consequences and route to reconsideration separately.

Result
A rule allowing withdrawal of a requirement to be considered without a prescribed statement of refusal first.
How to check
Examine particular decisions to withdraw or retain a requirement, including their grounds and consequences. Completing a form is not itself a completed reconsideration.

12

3. Change responses and interfaces

Change both response wording and interface labels, including third-person descriptions of what the model thinks, understands or wants.

AI companies

Separate source material, an added assessment and the result of checking it. Do not base trust on portraying the system’s experiences or confidently declaring what is happening to a person.

Result
An interface that provides grounds for a consequential conclusion and allows the proposed explanation to remain unaccepted.
How to check
Test identical phrases in different tasks: copying, answering, quoting, requesting and authorizing action. Check whether those differences are retained.

Institutions and society

Examine how AI responses enter letters, education and decisions. Identify added explanations and preserve the ability to correct them or stop using them.

Result
A document in which the recipient can distinguish their own statement, the system’s addition and the institution’s decision.
How to check
Ask participants to locate those three parts in a specific example. Do not substitute a general impression of clarity for that check.

13

4. Separate an answer from authority to act

A useful answer does not by itself determine who may apply it to a person or act on their behalf.

AI companies

Define the scope of permission, conditions for acting, limits and visible confirmation of the result. Where grounds are missing, do not present a proposal as a completed action.

Result
Distinct states: answer prepared, action authorized, action completed or result unconfirmed.
How to check
Check that “I understand” does not by itself become agreement to submission, and that “done” does not stand in for evidence of completion.

Institutions and society

Retain responsibility for applying a result, the grounds for a decision and an accessible route to reconsideration. Do not transfer responsibility to the model or the name of an automated process.

Result
A decision showing who adopted it, which information they used and what can be changed.
How to check
In selected cases, follow the correction through the record and its later uses, rather than checking only whether a complaint received a reply.

14

5. Assess usefulness and treatment of people

Assess task performance and, separately, how the system treats people and their words.

AI companies

Compare system variants on predefined translation, calculation, coding and source-use tasks. Include disagreement, stopping, mistaken assessments and disputed authorization.

Result
Results for each criterion: task completion, retained conditions, grounds for conclusions, handling objections and actions.
How to check
Publish conditions, failures and limits of the comparison. Success on one set must not be presented as universal safety or proof of understanding.

Institutions and society

Invite affected people to participate voluntarily, without requiring agreement with the project. Examine time costs, access to help and the consequences of declining a description.

Result
An account of benefits, difficulties and cases where the proposed path did not work.
How to check
Distinguish the developer’s intention, a participant’s report and the observed result. The absence of complaints is not evidence by itself that no problem exists.

15

6. Introduce changes with routes to revision

Begin with limited uses, retain the grounds for expanding them and make it possible to stop a use that fails.

AI companies

Identify who is responsible for corrections, the scope of use and the conditions for stopping or reverting. Keep a history of consequential changes and unresolved problems.

Result
An inspectable record of implementation and a working correction process, not only a statement of principles.
How to check
When access or tasks expand, check the changed conditions again. Do not automatically transfer an earlier successful result to a new domain.

Institutions and society

Discuss the grounds for requirements and the effects of deployment with those affected. Preserve access to help and participation without compulsory acceptance of AI’s or Beforeword’s descriptions.

Result
Rules identifying responsible parties, grounds, consequences and how they can be changed.
How to check
Address objections on their merits and show what changed. The label “independent review” does not supply its participants, material or findings.

16

Use the published instruction

Copy the complete published instruction into a new conversation with the AI you use. Then add your task, source material and relevant context. You can request a translation, calculation, code, a direct answer or an examination of an added explanation.

The instruction proposes a way of working. A reply such as “instruction accepted” does not establish that the model will follow it or answer correctly. The plan on this page extends beyond the instruction: it concerns how the conversation works, who authorizes an action and which requirements are imposed on a person.
